## Resolved Production Issues

### 1. Distinct Doors (No Flat Box)
- **Problem:** Closed doors appeared as a single flat white rectangle without visible separation.
- **Solution:**
  - Preserved exact mathematical PartGraph dimensions and 2 mm perimeter and inter-door reveals.
  - Added subtle edge definition (`THREE.EdgesGeometry` with `LineSegments` and opacity `0.35`) to each door leaf.
  - Aligned camera to front-facing perspective (`rotY = Math.PI - 0.42`), rendering contact shadows across the 2 mm door reveal gaps.
  - Result: `DOOR_01`, `DOOR_02`, `DOOR_03`, and `DOOR_04` are immediately readable as 4 distinct, separate door leaves.

### 2. Door Pivots & Hinge Geometry
- **Problem:** Doors lacked pivot groups and could not open around their hinge edges.
- **Solution:**
  - Created a dedicated `THREE.Group` pivot for every door:
    - `DOOR_01`: Left-hinged, pivot at $X = \min X$, $openY = +\pi \times 0.55$
    - `DOOR_02`: Right-hinged, pivot at $X = \max X$, $openY = -\pi \times 0.55$
    - `DOOR_03`: Left-hinged, pivot at $X = \min X$, $openY = +\pi \times 0.55$
    - `DOOR_04`: Right-hinged, pivot at $X = \max X$, $openY = -\pi \times 0.55$
  - Placed door meshes relative to their vertical hinge edge:
    - Left-hinged: local $X = +w/2$
    - Right-hinged: local $X = -w/2$
  - Rotating the pivot swings the door outward into the room around its physical hinge line—not its center.
  - Preserved original door mesh Part IDs (`part_DOOR_01`..`04`) and all placement metadata.

### 3. Builder Interaction Integration
- **Problem:** Door clicks and the "Open / close doors" button did not operate the generated wardrobe.
- **Solution:**
  - Registered all 4 door pivots directly into `Builder.doorObjs`.
  - Configured each pivot with the expected interaction fields:
    `{ interactive: true, kind: "door", openY, cur: 0, base: 0, hover: 0, suppress: 0 }`.
  - Updated `clickPick()` to support target delegation (`H.userData.pivot || H`), so clicking a door mesh in the 3D canvas toggles only that door's open state.
  - Existing `#toggle-doors` button toggles all four doors simultaneously.
  - Smooth animation lerp (`ud.cur += (tgt - ud.cur) * 0.16`) animates rotation in the existing animation loop.

### 4. Material Swatch Integration
- **Problem:** Material swatches did not update the parametric PartGraph wardrobe.
- **Solution:**
  - Created `updateParametricMaterial(builder, matKey)`.
  - Swatch clicks in the UI update the colors and PBR properties of the active PartGraph materials in place:
    - `materials.CARCASS`: color, roughness, metalness
    - `materials.DOOR`: matching door finish
    - `materials.PLINTH`: matching darker plinth tone
    - `materials.BACK_PANEL`: preserves semantic HDF backboard tone (`0xe5e1d5`)
  - Does NOT call `Builder.build()`, preventing incorrect legacy geometry substitution.
  - Preserves exact 19-panel PartGraph structure.

### 5. Internal Joinery & Shelf Inspection
- **Problem:** Internal wardrobe construction could not be inspected.
- **Solution:**
  - Opening the doors exposes the full interior:
    - Left Bay (Bay 1): 873 mm clear opening, upper fixed shelf, full hanging space.
    - Center Divider: 18 mm full-height partition separating both bays.
    - Right Bay (Bay 2): 873 mm clear opening, upper fixed shelf, 2 adjustable shelves.
    - Carcass Top, Bottom, and 50 mm recessed Plinth structure.
    - 6 mm back panel set into grooves.
  - Camera framing (`camDist = 4.8`) keeps the entire wardrobe and swinging doors framed comfortably within the viewport.
