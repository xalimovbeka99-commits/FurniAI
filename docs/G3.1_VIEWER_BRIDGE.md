# FurniAI G3.1 — Golden PartGraph in Existing 3D Builder

## Overview

Gate G3.1 connects the deterministic Golden Wardrobe `PartGraph v0.1` to the existing FurniAI production 3D Builder without modifying the 30-item catalog, creating a second canvas, or replacing the Three.js r128 renderer.

## Architecture

```
[goldenWardrobe.fixture.json]
             │
             ▼
   validateFurniSpec()
             │
             ▼
buildStructuralPartGraph()
             │
             ▼
    PartGraph v0.1 (19 panels)
             │
             ▼
   partGraphToThree()
   - 1 dmm = 0.0001 Three.js units (metres)
   - Coordinates: +X right, +Y upward, +Z rearward
   - Dimensions strictly from bounding box extents
   - Centers from ((min + max) / 2)
   - Complete metadata in mesh.userData
   - Complete recursive geometry & material disposal
             │
             ▼
  Existing Builder Scene & Canvas (#bld3d)
   - Reuses single WebGLRenderer
   - Reuses PerspectiveCamera & lights
   - Route: #/build/golden-parametric
   - Status badge: PARAMETRIC PARTGRAPH
```

## Coordinate System Mapping

| Parameter | FurniAI PartGraph v0.1 | Three.js World Space | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unit** | 1 deci-millimetre ($0.1\text{ mm}$) | 1 unit ($1.0\text{ m}$) | $\times 0.0001$ (`DMM_TO_THREE`) |
| **X Axis** | $+X$ Right ($0$ to $18000\text{ dmm}$) | $+X$ Right | Centered at $X = 0$ |
| **Y Axis** | $+Y$ Upward ($0$ to $24000\text{ dmm}$) | $+Y$ Upward | Bottom at floor ($Y = -1.201\text{ m}$) |
| **Z Axis** | $+Z$ Rearward ($0$ to $6000\text{ dmm}$) | $+Z$ Rearward | Centered at $Z = 0$ |

## Safety & Invariants

- **Catalog Preservation:** All 30 catalog designs (`DESIGNS[0..29]`) and routes (`#/build/0`..`29`) are untouched and functional.
- **Renderer Ownership:** Exactly one `THREE.WebGLRenderer` owns the `#bld3d` canvas.
- **Hardware Status:** Hardware drilling remains `BLOCKED_PENDING_HARDWARE_APPROVAL`.
- **Qualification:** CNC qualification remains `WORKSHOP_REVIEW_NOT_CNC_QUALIFIED`.
- **Zero Hallucination:** Geometry is derived purely from PartGraph; no dimensions are recalculated from spec.
